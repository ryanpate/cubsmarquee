# Quick Start — Cubs Marquee

Follow these steps to put your marquee on your home WiFi and get it showing scores. It
takes about 10 minutes, and you only do this once.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your marquee and its power supply (both in the box)
- A wall outlet
- Your home WiFi network name and password
- A phone, tablet, or laptop

## Steps

1. **Plug in the marquee**
   Connect the power supply to the marquee and plug it into a wall outlet.
   *You should see: the panel lights up within a few seconds. After about a minute it
   shows the word `SETUP` at the top, with instructions scrolling underneath. The very
   first time you power it on, the marquee restarts itself once along the way, so give it
   up to two minutes.*

   [PHOTO: the marquee showing the SETUP screen]

2. **Connect your phone to the marquee**
   In your phone's WiFi settings, choose the network named `CubsMarquee-Setup`. The
   password is `gocubsgo2024`.
   *You should see: your phone connects. It may warn you that this network has no
   internet — that is normal and you can ignore it.*

3. **Open the setup page**
   Open a web browser on your phone and go to `cubsmarquee.local/admin`.
   *You should see: a page headed `⚾ Cubs Scoreboard Admin` with a row of tabs across the top:
   `WiFi Setup`, `Display Config`, `System`, `Service Control`, and `Logs`.*

   If the page does not load, type `192.168.4.1` in the browser instead.

4. **Find your home WiFi**
   On the `WiFi Setup` tab, tap `Scan for Networks` and choose your home network from the
   list.
   *You should see: your network's name fill in the `Network Name (SSID)` box.*

   If your network is not listed, type its name into that box yourself.

5. **Enter your WiFi password**
   Type your home WiFi password into the `Password` box.

6. **Connect**
   Tap `Connect to WiFi`.
   *You should see: a confirmation message on the page. The marquee then restarts to join
   your network, and this page stops responding — that is expected.*

7. **Reconnect your phone to your home WiFi**
   Switch your phone back to your normal home WiFi network, then wait about two minutes.
   *You should see: the marquee stop showing the SETUP screen and start showing your
   team's scoreboard, upcoming game, or weather.*

   [PHOTO: the marquee showing a game or scoreboard]

## That's it

Your marquee is set up. Leave it plugged in — it starts on its own whenever it has power,
keeps itself up to date overnight, and needs nothing from you day to day.

**To set your local weather, change brightness, or pick what it shows:** go to
`cubsmarquee.local/admin` from any device on your home WiFi. See
[INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md).

**Something not working?** See Troubleshooting in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md).
